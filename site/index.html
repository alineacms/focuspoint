Two ways to find the point

The heuristic (about 4 kB) scores how cut off each region is from the image border, plus skin tone and a centre bias. The model replaces that importance map with a tiny learned network that knows a face matters more than a sign. Both then pick the subject, keep its most important spot whole, and place the point so typical crops keep it in view.

Measured on what people look at

On photos with faces, the model keeps the main head whole in 78–84% of crops, against 59–66% for the heuristic and 57–64% for a centred crop. On 55 hand-marked photos it keeps the marked point in 94% of crops. 35 kB, 4–6 ms per image.
